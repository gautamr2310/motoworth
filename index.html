Reliability context
MotoWorth does not treat a badge, country of origin or reputation as proof of long-term reliability. Model, year, maintenance and observed condition matter.
Brand perception can influence demand, but MotoWorth only converts that perception into a rupee premium or discount when used-market evidence supports it.
